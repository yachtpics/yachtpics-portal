A purpose-built portal where brokers manage every listing's media and share it as a clean, branded slideshow in seconds — from the desktop or their phone.

**Why it's different**
Built by yacht photographers, for yacht brokers. It understands a listing — vessel details, categories, slideshows, client sends — instead of being a generic Dropbox folder.

**The offer**
30-day free trial, no credit card. Plans from $29/mo. Viewing and downloading photos is always free.

**Tagline options (pick one as the lead)**
1. Every listing, client-ready in seconds.
2. Your listings, beautifully delivered.
3. From the dock to the buyer, in two taps.
4. From the dock, not the desk.

**Mobile is a headline benefit, not a footnote**
The portal installs on a phone like an app and runs full-screen. Brokers live at marinas and boat shows, not behind a desk — so "manage and send a listing right from the dock" is one of the strongest, most concrete selling points. Lead with it wherever the audience is mobile-first.
