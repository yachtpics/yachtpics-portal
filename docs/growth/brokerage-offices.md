# Selling the Portal to brokerage offices — South Florida

*Prepared September 12, 2026. Research by an Opus pass on public brokerage sites; pitch by Claude. Headcounts drift — recheck a team page before you walk in.*

## The one idea

Stop selling brokers one at a time. One sales manager is fifteen to forty brokers in a single conversation, and the office plan already exists in the portal. The pitch is not a feature list. It is: open **their** listing on your laptop, press Reel, hand them the phone.

## The room: FLIBS, October 28 – November 1

Six weeks out. Confirmed dates, Bahia Mar is the densest brokerage concentration and several targets office there year-round (HMY Bahia Mar, Allied Marine, Merle Wood, Galati FTL on Seabreeze). Superyacht Village at Pier Sixty-Six South is where N&J, Fraser, Burgess, IYC, Worth Avenue and Ocean Independence sit.

**The FLIBS play:** a card with a QR code that opens a reel of *a boat they have on display* — made that morning from your photos of it. Nothing to explain; the phone explains it. Print two hundred. Give the card to the sales manager, not the broker on the dock.

## What you're up against

Nothing. Opus found no yacht brokerage using a photo-delivery or reel tool. The competition for their marketing dollar is YachtWorld's Advantage tiers, YATCO's marketing arm and BoatWizard — listing exposure, not delivery. Denison publicly runs sponsored placements on "35+ MLS services." The portal is not replacing anything they pay for; it is the thing nobody sells them.

## The pitch

**The line:** *Your listings, delivered like this.*

**The email** (to the named contact; subject: `[Boat name] — a reel, 40 seconds`):

> Hi [First name],
>
> We photographed [Boat] for [Broker] last month. I made this from the shoot in about a minute — no editor, no agency: [link to reel]
>
> Every broker at [Brokerage] could do the same the morning their photos land: reels in six looks, a branded slideshow, a spec sheet, and a report of which photos each buyer actually lingered on. It's the YachtPics Portal, and the office plan covers everyone in the building.
>
> I'll be at Bahia Mar for FLIBS. Ten minutes on the dock with your phone is all it takes to see it. Which day suits?
>
> Charlie Clark · YachtPics · 561-602-9710

Keep it under 120 words. The reel does the selling; the email just delivers it.

**In person, on the dock or in the office** — the whole script is three moves:

1. Open one of their listings you've shot, on your laptop or phone. Press Reel. Hand it over while it renders. Say nothing.
2. When it finishes, press *Send to my phone* and let them scan it. Now it's on their phone, in their camera roll. "That's yours — post it."
3. Show Engagement on the same listing: "This is who opened it and which photos they stayed on." Then the price: one number for the whole office.

Do not walk through the six looks, the AI caption, brand colours or the safe-zone work. If they ask, show; if they don't, they've already decided.

**Objections you'll get, and the honest answers:**

- *"We already have a videographer."* — Good. This is for the twenty listings a year they don't shoot video on, and it's ready the same morning the photos are.
- *"Our brokers won't use another login."* — They already have one; every photo we've ever delivered came through it. The reel is one more button on a page they already open.
- *"What does it cost?"* — Name the office price once, clearly, and stop talking. Compare it to one listing's YachtWorld spotlight if they push.
- *"Can it push to YachtWorld?"* — No. Boats Group doesn't allow media partners and caps a listing at 50 MB combined. It goes to Instagram, Facebook, email and the client — where buyers actually are.

## The list you already own

Around two hundred listings in the portal means a couple of hundred brokers who have received photos from you. The announcement reaches all of them; the note that converts a lapsed trial is personal, one line, with a reel of *their* boat attached. Pull the list from Admin, sort by last sign-in, and send five a day. Samantha's Facebook habit — crediting the broker by name on every shoot — is the same move in public, and it is now built into every YachtPics-branded reel's end card.

## The first ten days

1. Copy the wordmark into the portal and make three YachtPics-branded reels (Energy on a centre console, Editorial on a motor yacht, Stack on a sportfish). Post one a day, tag the broker and the brokerage.
2. Email Dressler, Norcross, Todero, Perez, Kresge, Gyland and Balliette — one boat each, one reel each.
3. Order the FLIBS cards. Book the seven for ten minutes at Bahia Mar.
4. Send the announcement to the existing list once Stack is where you want it.
5. Ask the first office that signs for one sentence you can quote. Lead the Denison and N&J emails with it.
